Its comprehensive guide advertises a package with an initial assessment and ten sessions. That package is not a documented clinical treatment protocol; current pricing and availability have not been confirmed and are not added to the replacement website.

## Copy decision

The original website makes broad claims about wound healing, infection, inflammation, cognition, chronic conditions, pain, athletic performance and anti-aging. Those statements do not establish that SOZO's actual equipment and protocol support the claimed outcomes. None is carried over as a verified benefit.

The replacement page describes the advertised chamber-based service, retains the existing clinic image and layout, and turns the missing technical and protocol details into useful pre-booking questions. The shared services summary uses the same scope. It does not assert medical indications, regulatory clearance, a numerical oxygen dose or guaranteed results.

Manufacturer comparison and clinical matching remain unavailable without identifying the actual setup. No inference is made from chamber appearance, a similarly named clinic, or general HBOT marketing. New clinic-specific benefit claims should only be introduced after suitable equipment and protocol evidence is available.

## General medical HBOT education

The user subsequently reiterated that the chamber page must include benefits. General medical HBOT benefits are now presented separately from the clinic’s session information, based on Mayo Clinic’s patient information: https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/hyperbaric-oxygen-therapy/about/pac-20394380.

That source supports increased tissue oxygen delivery, growth of new blood vessels and tissue, and support for immune activity in appropriate medical treatment settings. The visible benefits section explicitly limits these benefits to prescribed medical HBOT protocols with clinical supervision and does not promise them from SOZO’s wellness sessions. This educational content does not verify SOZO’s equipment or establish that the clinic treats wounds, radiation injuries or infections.
