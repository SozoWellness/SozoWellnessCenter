name: Pricing management
description: User-selected editing workflow and approval constraints for Freedom credits and pricing.
---

Manage pricing through Replit updates, not a private staff CMS or admin screen.
The Freedom packages replace all legacy packages rather than supplementing them.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected “Updates through Replit” and “Replace all
old packages” after being offered a separate staff editor and a partial replacement.

**How to apply:** Keep prices and package details together in an editable catalog,
review changes in preview, and obtain approval before pushing to Cloudflare.
Do not introduce authentication, checkout, or a CMS merely to make pricing editable.

Do not infer credit redemption exceptions, expiration, sharing, or eligibility
rules from the posters. “Most services” using one credit is not an all-services
guarantee.

**Why:** The user said staff are still working on the terms and they are unknown.

**How to apply:** Require staff-approved terms before displaying specific rules;
until then, direct visitors to the team to confirm their selected services and terms.
