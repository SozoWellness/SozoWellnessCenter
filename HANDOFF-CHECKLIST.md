# SOZO — Handoff and Launch Checklist

Prepared October 30, 2026. For SOZO's internal web team.

**Your project contact will provide separate custom instructions for the forms and automations.**
Use this checklist to review the website and prepare for launch. It does not ask SOZO's team to design a technical setup.

## 1. Ownership and import

- [ ] SOZO controls the Replit account, billing and recovery details.
- [ ] Team members have individual access; no shared passwords are needed.
- [ ] Import the ZIP at <https://replit.com/import>; do not rebuild the site from scratch.
- [ ] Open the website preview and confirm the pages and images display.
- [ ] Share any import or setup error with your project contact rather than guessing.
- [ ] Keep passwords and access details out of shared documents.
- [ ] Tell your project contact if old enquiry records need to be retained. The ZIP contains none.
- [ ] Assign responsibility for maintenance, backups, access and ongoing hosting charges.

## 2. Connect the forms using the supplied instructions

### Current behavior

The included form is not the finished live process. It does not email staff, acknowledge the request by email, subscribe a contact to marketing or reserve an appointment. Importing the ZIP does not complete those connections.

### What to do

- [ ] Obtain the custom form and automation instructions from your project contact.
- [ ] Confirm the receiving inbox and who will respond to enquiries.
- [ ] Use the supplied instructions to complete the connections. Ask your project contact about anything unclear; do not invent a separate process.
- [ ] Send a test enquiry and confirm it reaches the intended inbox.
- [ ] Confirm the visitor receives the acknowledgment described in the supplied flow.
- [ ] Test with marketing selected and not selected; confirm each follows the supplied instructions.
- [ ] Confirm there are no unexpected welcome emails, duplicate messages or duplicate subscriptions.
- [ ] Agree on who checks for failed deliveries and who can access enquiry information.
- [ ] Update the website's form notice only after the new process is working.

Keep direct text/call booking instructions unless an actual appointment-booking process is separately approved. A request must not be described as a reserved appointment.

## 3. Finish the Perfect Day Guide

- [x] Obtain the separate custom instructions for guide signup and delivery: first name/email, immediate PDF access, emailed link, optional separate marketing consent, no texts.
- [x] Obtain the supplied PDF and SOZO's Google Drive sharing link.
- [ ] Approve the draft sender and email copy in `PERFECT-DAY-DELIVERY.md`.
- [ ] Follow those instructions to connect the signup and email flow.
- [ ] Test a new signup and confirm the correct guide arrives.
- [ ] Test an existing subscriber and a later repeat request as described in the custom flow.
- [ ] Verify unsubscribe handling and sender details.
- [ ] Replace the homepage's “coming soon” status only when signup and delivery work.

## 4. Review the imported website

- [ ] Ask the person handling implementation to confirm the imported site works correctly.
- [ ] Check desktop and mobile navigation, including Services and Articles & Resources.
- [ ] Check each of the four How We Help pathways and its destination.
- [ ] Confirm Book Your Assessment remains the main CTA and retains the chosen service.
- [ ] Confirm the phone number is visible and phone links work on a mobile device.
- [ ] Check all images, local testimonials and article Markdown downloads.
- [ ] Confirm Darcelle appears first and team profile links lead to the correct pages.
- [ ] Keep Chiropractic separate from Kevin Stasney, MD's Independent Physician page.
- [ ] Verify external partner/affiliate, map and Years Younger MD links independently; those external sites are not transferred by the ZIP.
- [ ] Obtain final clinic approval of prices, availability, service claims, testimonials, photos and policies.
- [ ] Review privacy/disclosure text against the actual forms, marketing and tracking installed at launch.
- [ ] Do not describe Meta Pixel as active unless it is actually installed and approved.
- [ ] Confirm no sensitive health information is requested through public forms.

## 5. Final checks before launch

- [ ] Have the person handling launch publish a review version in SOZO's account.
- [ ] Test the final forms and emails on that version before accepting real enquiries.
- [ ] Keep passwords and integration access details private.
- [ ] Confirm someone is responsible for responding to enquiries and checking delivery problems.
- [ ] Open important pages directly and refresh them, not just via navigation.
- [ ] Have the person handling implementation verify search-page information and missing-page behavior.
- [ ] Confirm the independent practice's medical appointments still go to Years Younger MD.

## 6. Switch to the new website

Have the person who manages the website/domain handle these steps with SOZO's approval:

- [ ] Record and back up the current website and DNS settings.
- [ ] Make sure links to old website pages lead to the relevant new pages. Importing the ZIP does not set this up.
- [ ] Confirm the final website address is `https://sozorootedwellness.com` and that the www version also works correctly.
- [ ] Connect the domain using the new project's Publishing instructions.
- [ ] Preserve clinic email settings when connecting the domain.
- [ ] Test the secure website address, old links, new pages, enquiry delivery and clinic email after the switch.
- [ ] Keep old hosting and the original project available until the new site is verified.
- [ ] Have the responsible person update Google's website/search settings after launch.
- [ ] Separately review Google Business Profile website/service information. Website edits do not update that profile automatically.

## Handoff acceptance

- [ ] SOZO can access and maintain the imported project without the previous owner's account.
- [ ] Remaining launch items have named owners and are not assumed complete.
- [ ] SOZO has approved the site and the final domain switch.

The ZIP itself does not change the live website, connect automations or send emails. The supplied custom instructions and final testing are still required.
